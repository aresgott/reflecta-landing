How it works

Answer in your words. Get insight back.

  1. 01Choose a reflection

    Start with what’s on your mind. Each assessment shows its time and credits up front, like Emotional Needs at about 15 minutes.

  2. 02Answer honestly

    Clear questions, one at a time. Take it solo, or invite a partner in.

  3. 03Say it your way

    When none of the options fit, describe what’s happening in your own words. The AI helper takes it from there.

  4. 04See what’s beneath

    A clear headline insight, the patterns that kept showing up, and where to put your energy next.
