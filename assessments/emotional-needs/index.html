You tell someone about a hard day and they jump straight to advice. They mean well, and you feel more alone. Emotional Needs shows which needs matter most to you, like being heard, reassurance, space or practical help, so you can ask for them.

What you need, and when.

Being heard

02

Reassurance

03

Space

Practical help

What you get back

Your needs, in plain words.

  • A headline insight that names what you need most.
  • What kept showing up across your answers, in your own words.
  • Where to put your energy next, like how to ask for what you need.
  • Take it together and see what each of you needs from the other.
