How do I show love, and how do I want it back?

You fill their car with gas and fix the sink. They say they never hear that you care. You're both loving hard, just in different languages. Love Languages shows how you give love and how you most like to receive it, and it works well taken together.

What it looks at

How love moves, both ways.

01

Words of affirmation

You might rarely say it out loud and still need to hear it often. How much words carry, given and received.

02

Quality time

Whether undivided attention is what you offer, what you quietly wait for, or both.

03

Acts of service

Caring by doing, and whether help feels like love when someone does it for you.

04

Gifts

The small things you pick out for others, and whether receiving one feels personal or just nice.
