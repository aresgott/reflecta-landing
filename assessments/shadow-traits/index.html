What it looks at

The parts of you that move first.

01

Defensiveness

How quickly you guard yourself when something sounds like criticism.

02

Pride protection

What you do, often without noticing, to avoid looking wrong, small, or not enough.

03

Emotional awareness

How well you can name what you feel while it's happening, not just afterwards.

04

Triggers

The kinds of moments that set your reactions off, and the older feelings they tend to touch.

What you get back

Your reactions, in plain words.

  • A headline insight that names what's going on for you.
  • What kept showing up across your answers.
  • How you're wired, across the parts of you that react first.
  • Where to put your energy next, so there's a little more room before the snap.
